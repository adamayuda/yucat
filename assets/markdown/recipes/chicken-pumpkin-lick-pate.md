# Chicken & Pumpkin Lick Pâté

*Soft and spoonable — for fussy eaters, older cats, and anyone who has tried to give a cat a tablet.*

![A smooth orange puree served in two bowls](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1476718406336-bb5a9690ee2a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)
*The texture to aim for: smooth enough to fall off a spoon, thick enough to hold its shape. Serve yours plain, without the garnish shown here.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Makes** | 8 servings of 1 tbsp |
| **Prep / Cook** | 10 min / 20 min |
| **Difficulty** | Easy |
| **Keeps** | 3 days chilled, 1 month frozen |

---

## Why it earns its place

- **Texture** — suits cats with dental pain or missing teeth
- **Moisture** — carries a lot of water into a species that drinks badly
- **Camouflage** — the most reliable way to hide a crushed tablet, if your vet confirms the drug may be given with food

---

## Ingredients

- **200 g chicken breast**, skinless and boneless
- **2 tbsp plain pumpkin purée** — 100% pumpkin
- **3–4 tbsp of the cooking water**, reserved
- **1 tsp salmon or sardine oil** *(optional)*

> **Pie filling is not purée.** It contains sugar and often nutmeg, which is toxic to cats. The tin must list pumpkin and nothing else. Never use chicken from a roast dinner — cooking fat, salt, onion and garlic are all problems, and the last two are toxic.

---

## Method

1. **Poach the chicken** in unsalted water at a gentle simmer for 15–18 minutes, until no pink remains.
2. **Reserve 4 tbsp of the cooking water.** This is your flavour and moisture — never a stock cube.
3. **Cool completely.** Cats reject food above body temperature, and scalding is a real risk.
4. **Check for bone and cartilage** with your fingers before blending.
5. **Blend the chicken** with 2 tbsp of the cooking water to a coarse paste.
6. **Add the pumpkin** and blend smooth.
7. **Adjust with the remaining water**, a spoon at a time, until it falls slowly off a spoon.
8. **Stir the oil through** at the end. Do not blend it — heat and blades degrade omega-3s.

## Serving

![A cat eating from a bowl](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1558993457-4bc6ec2c3734?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)
*A wide, shallow dish keeps the whiskers out of the food.*

Start with **one teaspoon** to test tolerance, then build to a tablespoon.

| Cat weight | Treat allowance | Pâté per day |
|---|---|---|
| 3 kg | 16 kcal | 1 tsp |
| 4 kg | 20 kcal | 1½ tsp |
| 5 kg | 24 kcal | 1 tbsp |

## Storage

- **Fridge:** 3 days sealed · **Freezer:** 1 month, frozen in an ice-cube tray then bagged
- **Thaw overnight in the fridge** — never on the counter, never in a microwave, which creates burning hot spots

---

## Variations

- **Turkey** instead of chicken, identical method
- **Extra hydration:** loosen with more cooking water into a gravy
- **Sensitive stomach:** leave out the oil and halve the pumpkin

---

## Cautions

- **This is not a complete meal.** No taurine, no calcium balance, no vitamin premix. Cats fed plain chicken as a staple develop serious deficiencies.
- **More pumpkin is not better** — too much causes loose stools.
- **Ask your vet before using it to hide medication**, and before feeding it to cats with diabetes, kidney disease or pancreatitis.
- **Introduce over three or four days** for a sensitive stomach.
